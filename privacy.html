Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

RunZ is designed to be private and respectful of your data. We do not sell your data, and the game contains no advertising and no third-party analytics.

What we collect

  • Nothing at all. Your progress and settings are stored only on your device. The game works fully offline and sends no usage data anywhere — there is no analytics SDK in the app.

Handled by Apple

  • Game Center (leaderboards) is provided by Apple under Apple’s own privacy policy, if you are signed in.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages (rated 4+). It contains no ads and no third-party content, and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Deleting the app removes all locally stored data from your device.
  • contact us via our contact form for any privacy request.
We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new date.
